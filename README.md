## Projects

### Suíte Edna
A modular platform, in development, for creating websites, landing pages, newsletters and banners from a shared component library. The AI acts as a composer: it selects, orders and fills existing components within scope rules for each content type, and every change is schema-validated and approved by a person.
`Next.js` `React` `Vite` `Tailwind` `Claude API`

### pArkse
A community-powered parking app: whoever leaves frees the spot, whoever searches finds it. MVP on iOS and Android, backend in production, full cycle validated in a street test in Porto. Anonymous, with reputation and spot prediction by zone and time of day.
`Expo` `React Native` `Fastify` `PostgreSQL/PostGIS` `Redis`

### PousArt
A 3D-printed wine table for the lawn, with brand, a waiting-list landing page and a launch plan in Porto. [pousart.pt](https://pousart.pt)
`Fusion` `Cinema 4D` `Vercel`

### Dia da MPB
Website for a Brazilian music festival in Porto, with the schedule read live from a spreadsheet and all content in a single configuration file. [diadampb.com](https://diadampb.com)
`React` `Vite` `Tailwind` `Vercel`

### OQCO news portal
News portal with its own CMS for O Que Cresci Ouvindo, server-rendered so every shared article shows the right title, image and summary on social networks. [oquecresciouvindo.com](https://oquecresciouvindo.com)
`Next.js` `Payload CMS` `PostgreSQL` `Vercel`
